Questions and answers

What is Carousel 7: Mishandled?

A first-person baggage sorting game for iPhone. You pick bags up, read their tags, check the board, and put each one down the right chute. What you earn buys skills.

What does it cost?

The game is free to download, and Receiving, the first area, is free for as long as you like. One purchase opens the other four areas. There are no subscriptions and nothing else to buy.

I bought the full game. How do I get it back on a new phone?

Open the unlock screen (tap the card reader by the roll-up door in Receiving) and choose Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.

Does anything happen while I am away?

No. Nothing is earned, nothing is lost, and no flight leaves while the game is closed. When you come back, the building is exactly as you left it.

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone. The game has no account and no cloud save, so deleting the app deletes your progress. Your purchase is kept by Apple and can always be restored.

A bag came back with a note. What did I do wrong?

It went down the wrong chute. The bag comes back and the note says where it should have gone. The board changes during a shift, so check it again.

Is Pine Hollow Regional a real airport?

No. The airport, the airlines, the flights, and the passengers are all made up.

Does the game use Game Center?

Yes, if you are signed in: for leaderboards and achievements only. The game works the same without it.
