Privacy Policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

The short version: Carousel 7: Mishandled does not collect, store, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, and, in the current version, no advertising.

Who we are

Carousel 7: Mishandled ("the game") is made by SquiresProduct. You can reach us through the contact form.

What the game stores, and where

Your progress (cash, skills, records, settings, and the state of the building) is saved on your device only. It is never sent to us. Deleting the game deletes it.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, location, or any other personal information, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Services provided by Apple

  • Purchases. The one-time purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the game only whether the purchase was made.
  • Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center, the game sends your scores and achievements to Apple so they can appear on leaderboards under your Game Center name. You can sign out of Game Center in the Settings app.

Apple handles this information under its own privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy.

Advertising

The current version contains no advertising and no advertising software. A later version may offer optional rewarded videos, shown only when you choose to watch one. If that happens, this policy will be updated first to name the advertising provider and say what it collects.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical details such as your IP address when you visit, under its privacy statement. The site itself uses no cookies and no analytics. If you use the contact form, what you send (including any screenshot) is delivered to us by FormSubmit, a form service, and we use it only to reply.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
